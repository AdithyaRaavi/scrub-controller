Scrub Arcade

One control, four games, all in 3D. Drag left or right to steer — wherever the cursor sits is the direction. Scrub it back and forth, fast, anywhere on the stage and that's your power — like working a scrubber. Ease off and you lose speed. No keyboard, ever.

🏎️

Redline Circuit

Five cars, five laps, one night circuit. Chase-cam 3D racing against four rivals who fight you for every corner.

🚣

River Row Sprint

Steer your boat down a winding 3D river and out-paddle three rivals to the flag. Dodge logs, clip buoys for a kick.

⛷️

Slope Rush

Carve a real downhill run against two rival skiers through a 3D mountain. Dodge trees and moguls or eat a crash penalty.

🌊

Slalom Jet Ski

Thread 14 buoy gates across open 3D water against two rivals. Miss a gate and the clock punishes you.
